### Slide 6: The Trust Pipeline: Verification Over Guesswork
**Headline:** The Trust Pipeline: Every Fact Is Verified, Never Guessed  
**Line 1:** 6-step flow: Document → OCR → AI Extraction → Confidence Score → Verification → Record  
**Line 2:** Transparent confidence ratings highlight every extracted value for review  
**Line 3:** Low-confidence or smudged words are flagged for user confirmation, never hallucinated  
**Line 4:** Structured records are finalized only after explicit patient verification  
*Footer:* Not medical advice. Consult your doctor.

**Speaker Notes:**
In healthcare technology, trust and factual accuracy are non-negotiable. HealthPilot implements a strict six-stage Trust Pipeline: Original Document, Optical Character Recognition, AI Extraction, Confidence Scoring, User Verification, and Structured Health Record. Every single entity extracted by our AI is accompanied by a confidence score. If an ink smudge or handwriting ambiguity causes uncertainty, the system never makes a blind guess. Instead, it flags the item with an attention banner and invites the user to verify or correct the information before it enters their permanent record. This human-in-the-loop guarantee eliminates AI hallucinations and keeps the patient in total control.

*Footer:* Not medical advice. Consult your doctor.

**Speaker Notes:**
A patient should never feel terrified by a confusing lab report they do not understand. HealthPilot translates complex clinical markers into warm, clear, plain-language summaries that anyone can grasp. For example, instead of just displaying an out-of-range flag, it explains: "Your hemoglobin is 10.8 g/dL, which is below the usual reference range. You may want to discuss this result with your doctor." Crucially, HealthPilot never attempts to diagnose illnesses or prescribe treatments on its own. Our language models are strictly prompted and safety-filtered to provide educational context that helps patients ask better questions during their actual doctor consultations.

### Slide 8: Grounded AI Health Chat: Verified vs. General Info
**Headline:** Grounded AI Chat: Record-Grounded vs. General Knowledge  
**Line 1:** Separates answers into "Based on your records" and "General health info"  
**Line 2:** Direct evidence drawer links answers back to original uploaded documents  
**Line 3:** Answers questions about past tests, medications, and upcoming visits  
**Line 4:** Guardrails detect emergencies and immediately advise professional medical care  
*Footer:* Not medical advice. Consult your doctor.

**Speaker Notes:**
Our AI Health Chat provides empathetic, interactive assistance with built-in attribution and transparency. When a user asks a question like "When was my last cholesterol check?", the chat clearly tags the answer under "Based on your records" and cites the exact test date. If they ask a general wellness question, the response is explicitly demarcated as "General health information." Users can click any record-based response to open the Evidence Drawer and view the exact source document snippet. Furthermore, safety guardrails automatically detect red-flag symptoms and urge the user to seek immediate emergency medical attention.
